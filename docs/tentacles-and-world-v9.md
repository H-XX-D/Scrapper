# V9: tentacles, pursuit, frame pacing and world repairs

Wall, floor and ceiling colonies now have four matching tentacle strains, with seeded reach from 2.6 to 5.1 metres. Mature colonies telegraph for 0.85 seconds and require clear line of sight. Their hook can hold a living salvager for at most 4.5 seconds. Seven changes of movement direction break the grip; keeping one direction held does not repeatedly advance escape. WASD, diagonal changes and the controller's left stick use the same host-authoritative input. Escaping grants three seconds of grab immunity and an eight-second colony cooldown. Small periodic damage makes rescuing a teammate useful.

During a grab, the camera pulls into a collision-checked third-person shoulder view. Rook, Echo, Flint and EOS each have eight new struggle, release and landing poses. The local body fades before the camera returns through it, and the gun/wipe are hidden during the restraint and short recovery. A teammate can shoot the root or the gripping hook. Killing the colony immediately releases the captive, plays recoil/rupture/husk frames and uses the strain's existing goo colours. Host state, guest prediction, network snapshots and save recovery handle the restraint. Loading a solo save clears a transient restraint safely. V9 uses room protocol 5; all participants should use V9.

World boundaries now subtract actual neighbouring openings on every deck, with four separate stair-edge samples per cell. A neighbour above or below the current room no longer removes its wall. Shallow duplicate stair landings are merged, duplicate coplanar cells are filtered, and the shaft floor is visually recessed 0.045 m under the lift's full-width platform. Closed or partly raised shutters block movement until there is enough headroom.

Unanchored room-bound accessory panels and oversized overhead decorations were removed. Existing station dressing continues to use validated wall anchors. Landmark rendering matches its bounded collision volumes. Mission devices and main terminals have solid bodies behind their clear interaction points; terminal screens stay on those bodies. Pressure plates and collectible couplers remain walkable intentionally. Route checks cover all twelve chapter families after installing machinery and infestation.

Bosses and mini-bosses latch pursuit after seeing the player, or after being damaged. They keep routing toward a living player beyond the ordinary enemy acquisition radius and around corners. Distant ordinary enemies avoid unnecessary sight traces. Pursuers share reverse path searches per destination, gate state and time window. Static compass/mission paths stop rebuilding while their inputs are unchanged. Sprite maps switch without invalidating the material shader, distant enemy animation work is culled, and network packet-size diagnostics no longer stringify every outgoing frame twice.

First-person weapon sizing uses each frame's real dimensions and last opaque row. The cut ends of the sleeves stay behind the existing HUD through reloads, bob and recoil; the weapon remains within the viewport.

## Validation

- 89 automated tests, including twelve chapter layouts with real collision/approach/path checks, vertical wall intervals, pursuit, all four tentacle strains on three surfaces, dodging the tell, bounded timeout, alternating escape and co-op destruction release.
- 64 new animation frames audited for missing artwork and canvas-edge clipping. Actual atlas gutters are authored in `src/grab-layouts.js`; generated equal-grid assumptions were corrected after visual review.
- 336 character/weapon/reload combinations checked at 1280×720, 1920×1080, 1024×768 and 2560×1080. Original suit recolouring and the single mixed-colour wipe remain covered by the existing regressions.
- Two local browser clients connected through the live PeerJS room service. A host-triggered floor grab appeared on the guest as Echo's third-person body; guest WASD inputs reached the host and escaped, with immunity replicated back. This tests live signalling and local peer transport, not separate household NATs. Test-only high health prevented unrelated enemy deaths during inspection.
- A 105-actor combat fixture used three destroyed pods and eighteen fan attacks. In the headless Chrome run at 1280×577, simulation update p95 fell from 26.2 ms (V8 export) to 3.5 ms (V9 export). In a sequential run using the same browser session, mean rendered frame time fell from 17.45 to 16.67 ms; both builds had a 16.8 ms p95 over 170 measured frames. The clearest improvement is the reduction in simulation spikes. These are observations on this machine, not a guaranteed frame rate on every device. Raw results and the fixture are in `artifacts/performance-v9.json` and `artifacts/benchmark-v9.js`.
- The numbered standalone HTML is rebuilt, checksum-verified and opened directly from disk before publishing. The release workflow independently rebuilds and verifies that same checksum.
