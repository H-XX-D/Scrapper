# Original wipe and expanded recovery story

The v4 claim that the original wipe had been restored was too broad. Its state tests proved a single clearing pass, but the renderer still selected `assets/legacy/visor-wipe-v18.png`, the later eight-frame sheet. V5 selects the original `assets/legacy/visor-wipe.png`: four poses, 2048 × 768, SHA-256 `3f0821d459c75484c6ca2e368cae8606789d463aa0ed1650478ec6c6a810c746`. The PNG is reused unchanged. No replacement hand was generated.

`wipeMotion` in `src/visor.js` lowers the current gun, raises one glove, sweeps from right to left once, withdraws it, and returns the gun in its supported grip. The gun is fully below the HUD throughout the glove's visible interval. There is no return sweep, hand shake, smear mechanic or required second pass. Captured stains clear as the sweep passes; a new hit during the animation survives. Existing blood sprites are composited only inside the glove's alpha during the two contact poses. The clean raise/withdraw poses stay clean. The existing sleeve recoloring supports all four salvagers.

The visual checks inspect the actual rendered glove and the runtime atlas rather than accepting the word “normal” in a state snapshot as proof. The alpha audit covers four poses for four pilots and eight palettes (128 variants): all nonempty, no clipped source edges, no alpha outside the original glove, all contact colors visible. Minimum source margin is three pixels before padding. Contact, sweep and return screenshots captured from the exact exported HTML are recorded under `artifacts`. A native Q-key check cleared all five colors, and the two-browser room check observed all four original poses on a guest with the gun hidden throughout the visible glove interval.

The story now has twelve expanded recovery files in `src/campaign-story.js`, integrated by `src/campaign.js`. Rook's first contract omits a crew list; the evidence follows an ignored biological recall, contaminated carrier gel, false inspection records and automatic dispatch authority. Echo handles the union and rescue traffic, Flint the clean repair effort, and EOS the preserved evidence and signal checks. Rook's salvage license, invoices and return-route habits remain central as he becomes an infestation specialist.

The existing RECOVERY JOURNAL holds the longer background and recovered evidence. Evidence unlocks when the chapter's control archive is actually recovered; research and power decisions append their consequences. Mission communications remain in the existing compass dropdown, with the same orange portrait. Puzzle completion, secret discovery, terminal choices and guardian aftermath use chapter-specific logs. Later deployment intros recall whether prior research was preserved or purged. Endings reflect the research balance, maintenance decisions and specific recovered chapters. An early purge records its order instead of claiming the ledger was already copied.

Radio messages now queue in arrival order. A decision can interrupt a field log, which resumes from its previous elapsed time after the choice. A second progress message no longer discards the current log. Radio history and interrupted-message timing persist in saves and room frames. Existing saves remain valid. Room protocol 4 requires v5 on all peers because the wipe presentation and narrative state changed.

The original title artwork, HUD layout, four-pilot cosmetics, low ceilings, procedural maps, props, weapons and combat systems remain. The existing ending panel scrolls internally to fit the longer closing report; no new story dialog or menu design was introduced.

Verification distinguishes scopes: unit tests cover motion, sequencing, narrative branching and journal gating; browser interactions solve all twelve chapter puzzle sequences, recover their archives and choose alternate research outcomes using verification teleports and enemy-damage hooks. These are integration checks, not a claim of a full human campaign playthrough. Exact-file save/load and multiplayer checks are recorded separately in the final verification JSON.

Dependency tracing covered visor → atlas → overlay, campaign → interactions/progress → radio/journal/ending, state serialization → saves/room frames, and export/preview consumers. The earlier game project was used only as a reference; no code in it was edited.
